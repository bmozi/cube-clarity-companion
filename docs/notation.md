# How do I read the moves and hold the cube?

John Briggs · November 25, 2026

Choose yellow up and green front for the examples. The common scheme here has white down, blue back, orange right and red left. Use your own puzzle’s actual scheme if it differs. A 2×2 has no center stickers; its course explains how to establish the frame from one corner.

U, D, R, L, F and B mean the up, down, right, left, front and back faces. Judge clockwise while looking straight at the named face. For D, imagine looking up at the underside; for L, look at its outside from the left. Keep the stated front while performing a sequence.

A bare letter is one clockwise quarter turn, an apostrophe (`'`, also printed ′) reverses it, and 2 is a half turn. Read left to right; complete a half turn before marking its move finished. `R U R'` contains three turns. To undo it, reverse order and direction: `R U' R'`. A half turn is its own inverse.

Lowercase r/l/u/d/f/b means the named outside layer together with its adjacent middle slice. M turns the middle layer between L and R in L’s direction; E follows D and S follows F. Whole-cube x follows R, y follows U and z follows F. A whole-cube rotation changes your frame; an ordinary U turn does not change front. A bar between groups is a reading separator, not a move, a regrip instruction or a restoration promise.

An address lists the faces meeting at a place: UFR is the upper-front-right corner; UF is the upper-front edge; DFR and FR form the ordinary front-right pair home. In blindfold lessons a sticker address begins with its own facing face; UF and FU are different stickers on the same edge. URF and UFR identify the same corner location; the blindfold map specifies individual stickers.

Before practising, try a turn and its inverse. The starting pattern must return even on a mixed cube. Keep the layers aligned and use a comfortable grip; stop if turning causes pain. Do not force a layer or physically twist a piece to imitate an impossible-state example.

Optional official notation reference: [WCA Article 12](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/regulations/#article-12-notation). The [2×2 course](2x2-course.md) includes its complete solving instructions.
